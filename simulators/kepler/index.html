Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion

Mathematical Framework of Celestial Mechanics

1st Law: Law of Ellipses

The orbit of a planet is an ellipse with the Sun located at one of the two foci.

$$r(\theta) = \frac{a(1 - e^2)}{1 + e \cos\theta}$$

2nd Law: Law of Equal Areas

A line segment joining a planet and the Sun sweeps out equal areas during equal intervals of time due to conservation of angular momentum $L$.

$$\frac{dA}{dt} = \frac{L}{2m} = \text{constant}$$

3rd Law: Law of Harmonics

The square of the orbital period $T$ of a planet is directly proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis $a$ of its orbit.

$$T^2 = \left(\frac{4\pi^2}{G M_\odot}\right) a^3 \implies \frac{T^2}{a^3} = 1.00 \quad [\text{Solar Units}]$$
